Q&A

What happens on the intro call?

A free 15-minute call to get familiar with your player and your family, and see if it's a good fit. Prices for 1:1 coaching calls are covered on the call. If it's a fit, I'll send a secure link for your 1:1 coaching calls. One free intro call per player.

What to share before a call?

Your player's team, age group and jersey number. There's a spot for each when you book, and I'll prepare a short preview of what to expect on the coaching calls.

Where does the footage come from?

LiveBarn, and I pull it myself, so you don't need a subscription. If a game isn't on LiveBarn, I may ask you for team video or your own recording.

Can we reschedule?

Yes, up to two times, with at least three days' notice before your call. Unused calls never expire.

What if we miss the intro call?

You'll get an email with a link to book a new time.
