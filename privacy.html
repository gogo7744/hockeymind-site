Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Hockey Mind is run by Max Golod. This policy explains what information I collect when you visit hockeymind.ca, book a call, fill in a form on Facebook or Instagram, or buy coaching, and how I use it.

What I collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Details about your player that you choose to share, such as their team, age group and jersey number.
  • Booking details (the time you choose) and payment confirmation. I never see or store your card details.
  • Basic website analytics, such as which pages were visited, through the Meta Pixel.

How I use it

  • To contact you about your free intro call and your coaching.
  • To prepare for calls and find your player's game footage.
  • To send booking links, reminders and receipts.
  • To understand which ads bring visitors to the site.

I don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Services I use

Your information is handled by the services that run Hockey Mind: Calendly (booking), Google (calendar, email and video calls), Stripe (payments), Skool (the Hockey IQ Course), Make (automated emails) and Meta (Facebook and Instagram ads and forms). Each has its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask me to see, correct or delete your information at any time, or stop receiving messages, by emailing max@hockeymind.ca.
